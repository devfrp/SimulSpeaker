Real speakers. In a real room. On your headphones.

Speaker Sim turns a pair of measured loudspeakers into a virtual audio output: their CEA-2034 response, the reflections of a room, a KEMAR head, and an AutoEq correction for your exact headphone model. Native on Linux through PipeWire, exportable everywhere else.

02How it works

Five stages are baked into four minimum-phase impulse responses — left/right speaker to left/right ear — then run by PipeWire's convolver in a separate, user-level process.

Speaker

Listening Window for the direct sound, Early Reflections and Sound Power for what the room returns.

CEA-2034 · spinorama

Room

Real materials and acoustic treatment, absorption per octave band, RT60 per band (Eyring), and image sources filtered by every wall they hit.

RT60 / octave · Eyring

Head

Each arrival is rendered through a KEMAR HRTF from its true direction, with exact fractional delays and diffuse-field EQ.

libmysofa · SOFA

Headphone

Your model's AutoEq parametric EQ, turned into a minimum-phase impulse response.

AutoEq

Output

A virtual sink becomes your default output; stopping it restores the previous one, even if systemd stops it.

PipeWire · systemd --user
IR[s][e] = HP⁻¹ · ( DF⁻¹ · ( LW · Direct[s][e] + ER · Reflections[s][e] ) + SP · Reverb[s][e] )

04FAQ

Short answers. The README has the long ones.

What does it write on my system?

Settings and filters in ~/.local/state/devfrp-speaker-sim, measurements in ~/.cache/devfrp-speaker-sim. Enabling auto-start asks systemd to link the unit, which creates symlinks under ~/.config/systemd/user; disabling removes them.

What does the acoustic treatment rate change?

It is the share of the walls covered with absorbing panels. Panels soak up the mids and highs much more than the bass, so the RT60 meters drop above 250 Hz first; add bass traps to tame the low end. Six presets go from a bare room to a very dead control room.

Why is it quieter than direct playback?

Filters are normalised for the worst case — the same signal on both speakers — so nothing ever clips. Raise the preamp if your material leaves headroom.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for anything already in the cache. Sources are pinned to a Git commit, so a cached file never changes and is never downloaded twice.

Why a generic KEMAR head?

It is the reference most measurements are built around. Any SOFA file can be set as sofa_file in the configuration if you have your own HRTF.

How do I update or uninstall on Linux?

Re-run the curl command to update. Append “bash -s -- --uninstall” to remove the app (settings kept) or “--purge” to remove settings too.

Where does the data come from?

AutoEq by Jaakko Pasanen (MIT), Spinorama by Pierre Aubert (GPL-3.0) with ASR Klippel NFS measurements, and the MIT Media Lab KEMAR set. Data is fetched on demand, not redistributed.
